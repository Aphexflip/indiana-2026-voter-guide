Methods and sources. Polling leads are taken from the independent model publisher’s source-weighted poll averages, only when polling is available; they are not certified vote totals. Projections are the publisher’s simulations, which can disagree with polling leads. Model API: Polling Forecast (CC BY 4.0) ↗. Source values are labelled with their own as-of date.

2026 election returns, where available, are unprojected counts from state election offices as relayed by Open America ↗. Its current feeds cover only 22 states and do not call races or certify winners. The site will say “counting,” “unavailable,” or “no confirmed winner” as appropriate. State return feeds are not nationwide. House results include individual districts, not a single statewide House winner. To verify an election call or certification, consult the relevant election authority or a named race-calling news organization.
