METHODOLOGY

Show the record. Link the source.

The site is designed to make political claims easier to inspect, not to tell readers what conclusion to reach.

01

No source, no claim.

Record items need a primary source or an established secondary source. Direct government records, roll-call votes, bill text, official transcripts, and direct quotations are preferred.

02

Separate facts from ratings.

If a third party publishes a score or characterization, the site attributes it to that organization rather than adopting it as its own judgment.

03

Flag uncertainty.

If a candidate, result, or record item has not been independently verified, the page says so instead of filling the gap with an assumption.

04

Same standard across candidates.

Comparable candidates should receive comparable sourcing depth, tone, and scrutiny. Missing records are labeled as missing rather than silently omitted.

What this site does not do

It does not endorse candidates, assign winners, score political choices, or predict election outcomes. The useful part is the evidence trail: claim → source → reader judgment.
