METHODOLOGY

Claim.
Source.
Context.

The point is not to tell you what conclusion to reach. The point is to make the underlying record easier to inspect.

01

No source, no claim.

Primary sources are preferred: government records, roll-call votes, bill text, official transcripts, court records, and direct quotations.

02

Attribution stays attached.

If a third party publishes a score, label, or characterization, the site attributes it to that organization instead of adopting it as its own judgment.

03

Uncertainty stays visible.

If a candidate, result, or record item is not verified, the gap is labeled rather than filled with an assumption.

04

Comparable treatment.

Comparable candidates should receive comparable sourcing depth, tone, and scrutiny. Missing material is marked as missing instead of quietly ignored.

What this site does not do

It does not endorse candidates, rank political choices, assign winners, or predict election outcomes. Featured placement is for navigation only.
